## 🇬🇧 English

Two skills for public-company modeling and research, covering both the numbers and the read. finance-model-builder runs Chinese-language, auditable, machine-gated three-statement forecasts, DCF, LBO or comparable-company valuations on A-share, Hong Kong and US listed companies, supporting incremental search of the latest filings, evidence freezing of corporate actions such as ex-dividend and stock dividends, revenue growth plus multi-product volume-price forecasting, three-statement linkage, FCFF/WACC/terminal value, tranched debt and returns, peer screening and relative valuation — for forecasts, budgets, target prices, leveraged returns and deal comparables, not for order execution, pure credit ratings or M&A legal opinions. public-company-analysis covers business model, competitive advantage, industry position, financial quality, valuation, risk and investment view, tagging every precise number with a source, rewriting missing key inputs as readable scenario tiers instead of hard assumptions, and opening with an auditable summary of no more than five lines. Used together, the analysis skill frames the company and industry, then the modeling skill lands that judgment in three statements and valuation.

> ⚠️ **A cooperating suite of 2 skills** — they reference each other; use them as a group.

### ✨ What it does

- Three-statement forecast: revenue growth and multi-product volume-price splits, statement linkage, machine-blocking quality gates and a non-bypassable conclusion gate
- Valuation set: DCF with FCFF/WACC/terminal value, LBO tranched debt and returns, and comparable-company relative valuation
- Evidence freezing: incremental search of the latest filings with auditable records of corporate actions such as ex-dividend and stock dividends
- Company deep dive: business model, competitive advantage, industry position, financial quality, valuation, risk and investment view
- Auditable output: every precise number carries a source tag, and missing key inputs become scenario tiers instead of silent assumptions or refusals
- Explicit boundary: no order execution, no pure credit ratings, no M&A legal opinions, with resource isolation during execution
